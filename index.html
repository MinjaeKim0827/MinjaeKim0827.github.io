About

I am a physics undergraduate at KAIST (B.S. expected February 2027, minor in Electrical Engineering). Since joining Prof. Junhan Kim's OBSCAL group in Fall 2024, I have modeled the X-ray emission of hot gas around galaxies in IllustrisTNG, and since January 2026 I have worked at KASI with SPHEREx data, measuring the rest-frame optical spectra of quasars at z > 5.

My long-term goal is to become an observational astronomer who works across observations, simulations, and instrumentation, connecting multi-wavelength data from X-ray to radio with theoretical predictions.

  • Circumgalactic medium
  • Galaxy evolution & feedback
  • High-redshift quasars
  • Survey spectroscopy
  • X-ray & near-infrared diagnostics
